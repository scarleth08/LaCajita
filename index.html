Our Story

We are a small jewelry business based in Quito, Ecuador. We source our pieces from local jewelers who work with talented artists, using traditional techniques to create unique designs that tell a story. Occasionally, a special piece from Italy joins the collection too. Most of our pieces are limited designs crafted in 925 sterling silver and 18k gold.
